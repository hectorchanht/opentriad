Open the underworld — take it apart, and see why it fascinates.

A preservation archive first, an explainer second: the screen canon is the door, the archive is the house. Educational and historical throughout — never a how-to, never glamour without the cost.

What it is

The closed world of organised crime, opened: a free cultural-history explainer in the Dawn Open* pattern. The folk knowledge is dying — 老聯、塘口、天打雷劈（誓詞）and the rest of the oral tradition survive only inside people's memories. OpenTriad rescues that record as history, published from Canada, and lays the complete set out discovery-first: the whole map on one surface, every card linked to its neighbours.

World map — 香港三合會 · 意大利黑手黨 · 日本極道 · 美國 · 台灣 · 阿姆斯特丹 Fact vs fiction — every card labelled The screen canon — 古惑仔 · The Godfather 典故詞彙庫 — 三刀六口 and the lost glossary 人物誌 — real figures, fictional icons, labelled

END GOAL

Rescue the vanishing oral and folk record — 洪門詩句、三十六誓、切口、茶陣、職級、塘口 — before the last generation that remembers it is gone, and keep opening the map of the whole earth, chapter by chapter, forever: every theatre's origins, structure, ranks, rituals and codes, explained historically, with the myth and the history never passed off as each other.

「我哋文字嘅根，唔可以畀佢唔見咗。」— the roots of our written culture must not be allowed to disappear.

  5. Quizzes and interactivity — phase two, on top of the reading archive of chapters, cards and timelines.
